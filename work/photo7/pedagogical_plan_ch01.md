- **O04** [interpret/understanding] Interpret the data in Quantitative Experimental Data: Action Spectrum vs Absorption Spectrum. (covers: C026)
- **O05** [explain/understanding] Determine the theoretical moles of glucose produced if the experimental quantum requirement is $8\text{ photons per molecule of }\text{O}_2$ evolved. (covers: C012)
- **O06** [explain/understanding] If the thylakoid membrane becomes permeable to protons due to a chemical ionophore, what is the immediate biochemical consequence?. (covers: C022)
