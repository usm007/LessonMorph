# Visual Design System

## Projector-Optimized Specifications

Classroom presentations must be legible from the back of a large room under varied lighting conditions:

- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 widescreen (13.333 inches × 7.5 inches).
- **Minimum Font Sizes**:
  - Hero Titles: 36–40 pt bold
  - Slide Titles: 28–32 pt bold
  - Body Text: 16–18 pt
  - Formula Notation: 24–28 pt
  - Minimum Badge/Note: 11–13 pt bold
- **Color Contrast Semantics**:
  - Hero Background: Deep Slate `#0F172A`
  - Slide Canvas: Off-White `#F8FAFC`
  - Cards: Pure White `#FFFFFF` with Slate Border `#E2E8F0`
  - Text Primary: Dark Slate `#0F172A` (ensuring 12:1+ contrast ratio)
  - Common Mistake / Warning: Rose Red `#E11D48`
  - Verified Correct Reasoning: Emerald Green `#10B981`
  - Brand Accent: Ocean Sky `#0284C7`
